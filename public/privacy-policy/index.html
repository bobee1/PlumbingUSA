Privacy Policy

This policy covers the personal information PipeMend handles when you ask for help with a plumbing problem, and the choices you have about it.

Document: PipeMend Privacy Policy · Effective November 19, 2026 · Owner and operator: Anjam Javed, doing business as PipeMend (pipemend.us)

1. Scope

This policy applies to pipemend.us, the PipeMend phone line (1-800-555-0199), and texts or emails sent about a plumbing request. "PipeMend", "we", "us" and "our" mean Anjam Javed, doing business as PipeMend. "You" means any person who visits the site, calls, texts or submits a form.

2. What we collect

Details you choose to share

  • Form submissions: name, phone number, ZIP code, your message, the page the form was sent from, and a timestamp showing when you agreed to be contacted.
  • Calls: the number you called from, call time and length, the property address or ZIP code, the plumbing problem, and anything else you tell us about the job. Calls may be recorded (section 5).
  • Texts and photos: messages you send, and any photos or videos of the leak, fixture or equipment you share so a plumber can plan the job.
  • Insurance and property details: if you choose to share them, your insurer's name and claim number, a landlord or property manager's contact details, or gate and access codes, so the job can be done and records sent to the right place.

Details collected automatically

  • Hosting and security data: Cloudflare, which hosts this site, processes IP addresses, browser details and requested pages to deliver the site and block abuse.
  • Visit counts: if analytics are turned on, we use a cookie-free tool that counts page views without tracking you across other websites.

Please do not send Social Security numbers, bank or card numbers, or medical records through this site. We do not ask for them online.

3. Why we use it

  • To answer your request and arrange plumbing repair, replacement or inspection work at the property.
  • To contact you by phone or text about scheduling, access to the property and job updates.
  • To prepare photos, notes and invoices that support a warranty or insurance claim, when you ask us to.
  • To review call quality, train staff and settle disputes.
  • To protect the site from fraud and fix technical problems.
  • To follow the law, including record-keeping duties and valid legal requests.

4. When we share it

  • The plumber doing your job: your name, phone number, address and job details go to the licensed local plumber assigned to your property. Some plumbers pay PipeMend a fee for the work we send them.
  • Your insurer, landlord or property manager: only when you ask us to send records or photos to them.
  • Vendors that help us operate: hosting (Cloudflare), email delivery (Resend), and phone and call-tracking services. They may use the data only to provide their service to us.
  • Permit offices: when a job needs a permit, the property address and job details go to the local building department as part of the application.
  • Legal duties: when required by law or court order, or to protect people, property or our rights.
  • Change of ownership: if PipeMend is sold or merged, records may transfer to the new owner, who must follow this policy.

Some state privacy laws treat passing contact details to a plumber for a fee as a "sale" of personal information. You may opt out of that at any time (section 8).

5. Recorded calls

We may record calls to 1-800-555-0199 for training, quality and records. Federal law (18 U.S.C. § 2511(2)(d)) permits recording with one party's consent. Some states require consent from everyone on the call, including California (Penal Code § 632), Florida (Fla. Stat. § 934.03), Illinois (720 ILCS 5/14-2), Maryland (Cts. & Jud. Proc. § 10-402), Massachusetts (G.L. c. 272, § 99), Montana, New Hampshire, Pennsylvania (18 Pa.C.S. § 5704) and Washington (RCW 9.73.030). So every call starts with a recording notice. Ask us not to record, and we will stop, or use the form instead.

6. Calls and text messages

Ticking the consent box on our form means you agree that PipeMend and the plumber serving your area may call or text the number you gave about your request, which may include automated calls or texts. This consent is collected under the Telephone Consumer Protection Act (47 U.S.C. § 227). You do not have to agree in order to buy anything. Reply STOP to end texts, or tell us on a call to stop calling. We never sell phone numbers to telemarketers.

7. How long we keep it

Job records, photos and call recordings are kept as long as we need them for the purposes in section 3, for warranty and billing questions, and to meet legal duties. After that we delete them or strip out the details that identify you.

8. Your choices and rights

Depending on your state, you may have the right to:

  • learn what personal information we hold about you and how it is used;
  • receive a copy;
  • have mistakes corrected;
  • have it deleted;
  • opt out of its "sale" or "sharing", and of targeted ads.

California residents hold these rights under the California Consumer Privacy Act as amended by the California Privacy Rights Act (Cal. Civ. Code § 1798.100 et seq.). Residents of Virginia, Colorado, Connecticut, Texas, Oregon and other states with consumer privacy laws hold similar rights. We give every U.S. visitor the same basic choices, whether or not their state requires it.

To make a request, call 1-800-555-0199 or use the contact form and say "privacy request". We verify you by matching the phone number or details on file, then respond within 45 days. An authorized agent may act for you. We treat a Global Privacy Control signal from your browser as an opt-out. Using your rights will not change how we treat you. If we turn down a request, reply to our answer to appeal, and we will respond within the time your state allows.

9. How we protect it

Safeguards include HTTPS encryption, limited staff access and vetted vendors. No system can promise perfect security. If a breach affects your personal information, we will tell you as your state's breach-notification law requires; every state has one.

10. Children's privacy

This site is meant for adults dealing with property damage. It is not aimed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect their information, consistent with the Children's Online Privacy Protection Act. If a child has sent us information, contact us and we will delete it.

11. Other websites

Our pages link to government agencies, utilities and other sources. Those sites have their own privacy practices, which we do not control.

12. Updates

When this policy changes, the new version is posted here with a new effective date. Major changes are flagged at the top of the page.

13. Questions
