What to do while you wait for the plumber

  1. Shut off the water. The main valve is usually where the water line enters the house: in a basement, crawl space, garage or utility closet. Turn a round handle clockwise. Turn a lever a quarter turn so it crosses the pipe.
  2. Keep clear of electricity. Do not stand in water near outlets or the electrical panel. Switch off a circuit only from a dry spot.
  3. Drain the lines. Open a faucet on the lowest floor to empty the pipes and relieve pressure.
  4. Protect the water heater. If the water will be off for a while, set a gas heater to "off" or turn off an electric heater's breaker.
  5. Move valuables and start drying. Mop, use towels and run fans. Photograph the damage first for insurance.

Burst and frozen pipes

Water expands as it freezes, which can split copper or plastic pipe. Pipes in exterior walls, attics, crawl spaces and garages are most at risk. The American Red Cross says to keep a faucet open while you warm a frozen pipe with a hair dryer, heating pad or warm towels. Never use a torch, kerosene heater or any open flame. If you cannot find or reach the frozen section, call. Many splits only show up once the ice melts and water flows again, so watch for new leaks after a thaw.

Sewer backups

When several drains back up at once, the blockage is usually in the main drain or sewer line, not a single fixture. The EPA lists fats, oils and grease, wipes labeled "flushable," and tree roots among the causes of sewer blockages. Stop running water, keep children and pets away, and do not use chemical drain openers on sewage. The EPA says areas flooded by sewage must be cleaned and disinfected, and that carpet, wallboard and upholstered items that touched sewage usually need to be replaced. A plumber clears the line, then runs a camera to find the cause. Repeated backups often point to sewer line repair.

Gas smell

A rotten-egg smell, a hissing sound or bubbles in standing water near a gas line can mean a leak. PHMSA, the federal pipeline safety agency, says to leave the area at once on foot, avoid anything that could make a spark, and call 911 from a safe distance. Then call your gas company. Once the utility has made the area safe, a plumber licensed for gas work can find and repair the leak and pressure-test the line.

Emergency plumber vs. water damage cleanup

A plumber stops the source and repairs the pipe, fixture or water heater. Large areas of soaked carpet, drywall or insulation often need a separate drying and cleanup crew. Ask your insurer which they want documented first. The Insurance Information Institute says standard homeowners policies cover burst pipes, but sewer backups are often excluded unless you added sewer backup coverage.

What affects the cost of an emergency plumber

  • Time of day: nights, weekends and holidays can cost more.
  • Access: pipes behind tile, in ceilings or under slabs take longer to reach.
  • Cause: a split supply line is quicker than a root-blocked sewer.
  • Equipment: camera inspection or hydro jetting adds equipment time.
  • Follow-up work: permits, drywall repair or pipe replacement may come later.

DIY or call a professional?

You can close valves, plunge a single toilet and thaw an exposed pipe with gentle heat. Call a plumber when water will not stop, when the leak is inside a wall or ceiling, when more than one drain backs up, or when gas or sewage is involved.

Emergency plumbing questions

What counts as a plumbing emergency?

Water you cannot stop, sewage coming up a drain, no water at all, a gas smell, or a leak near electrical wiring. A slow drain or a dripping faucet can usually wait for a weekday visit.

Do emergency plumbers charge more at night?

Often, yes. Nights, weekends and holidays can cost more than a weekday visit. You are told the price basis before work begins, and the full job is priced in a written estimate.

Can a plumber fix a burst pipe the same day?

Usually the leak can be stopped and the pipe repaired in one visit, often with a section of new pipe or a repair coupling. Pipe inside a finished wall may need the drywall opened, which is patched later.

Should I turn off my water heater during a plumbing emergency?

If the main water is shut off for more than a short time, or the water heater itself is leaking, turn the gas control to off or switch off its breaker. A tank that runs without water flowing in can be damaged.

Is sewage backup an emergency?

Yes. Sewage carries germs, and the EPA says areas flooded by sewage must be cleaned and disinfected. Stop using water in the home, keep people and pets away, and call.
