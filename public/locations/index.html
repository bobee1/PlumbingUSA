How plumbing differs across the country

A plumbing call in Boston looks different from one in Phoenix. These five patterns come from Census and FEMA data, and the states where each one stands out most.

PatternStates where it stands outWhat it means for plumbing
Oldest housing (most homes built before 1940)Washington, DC, Massachusetts, New York, Rhode Island, PennsylvaniaGalvanized pipe, cast-iron and clay drains; repiping and sewer work
Newest housing (latest median year built)Nevada, Arizona, Georgia, South Carolina, TexasFixture, water heater and fitting repairs; slab leaks in slab-built areas
Most FEMA water-related disasters since 2000Oklahoma, Kansas, Kentucky, Missouri, TennesseeFlooded basements, sewer backups and sump pumps
Most homes heated with natural gasUtah, Illinois, Michigan, New Jersey, ColoradoGas water heaters, venting and gas line work
Most homes heated with electricityFlorida, South Carolina, Alabama, North Carolina, TennesseeElectric and heat pump water heaters

Sources: Census ACS 2020–2024 housing and 2024 house heating fuel; OpenFEMA major disaster declarations from 2000 to November 2026.

Questions about where we work

Does PipeMend have plumbers in my state?

Yes. PipeMend takes plumbing calls in all 50 states and Washington, DC, including small towns and rural counties. Call 1-800-555-0199 any time, day or night.

Why do plumbing problems differ from state to state?

Housing age, climate, water hardness, soil and the main heating fuel all play a part. Older Northeast and Midwest homes have more galvanized pipe and cast-iron drains, cold states see frozen pipes, and slab-built homes in the South and West see slab leaks.

Who licenses plumbers in my state?

Most states license plumbers through a state board or agency. A few, including New York, Pennsylvania, Kansas, Missouri, Nebraska and Wyoming, leave plumber licensing to cities and counties. Each state page names the authority and links to it.

Do you have pages for my city?

City pages are added in waves, starting with the cities where the most people search for a plumber. Until your city has its own page, your state page covers it, and the phone line works everywhere.
